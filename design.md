# Min-UI Design & Copy Principles

## Principles
1. **Compact, Clear, and Task-Focused**:
   Keep the interface compact, clear, and focused on the user’s immediate task. Preserve accepted geometry, hierarchy, and content unless a change is required. Reuse existing UI primitives and patterns. Keep controls close to the content they affect. Show the effective state clearly.

2. **Concise Labels & Direct Instructions**:
   Use concise labels and direct instructions. Prefer verbs over nouns. Use one consistent term for each concept. State what happened and what the user should do next. Avoid marketing language, vague claims, unnecessary explanation, and rotating synonyms. Every sentence should make one clear claim.

3. **Design for Real Product Use**:
   Inspect the existing surface, states, and widths before changing it. Verify the result in the relevant workflow and report visual checks honestly.
